FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Our Faridabad Services

Do you provide interior design services across Faridabad? +

Yes, we serve all major regions across Delhi NCR including South Delhi, Dwarka, Vasant Kunj, Greater Kailash, Gurgaon, Noida, Ghaziabad, and Faridabad.

Do you take turnkey interior projects? +

Yes, we specialize in complete turnkey execution from initial architectural layout & 3D visualizations to on-site carpentry, custom furniture manufacturing, lighting, and final handover.

Do you visit the site before starting a project? +

Absolutely. Our team conducts detailed site visits to measure exact spatial boundaries, inspect structural conditions, evaluate natural light, and discuss design preferences directly with you.

Which areas in Delhi do you serve? +

We cover South Delhi, West Delhi, Central Delhi, North Delhi, East Delhi, Dwarka, Janakpuri, Greater Kailash, Vasant Vihar, Punjabi Bagh, Rohini, Saket, and surrounding upscale neighborhoods.

Do you work in Farmhouses and Villas? +

Yes, we have designed numerous sprawling country farmhouses, luxury villas, modern builder floors, and high-rise penthouses with custom interior architecture and solid wood joinery.

What is the typical timeframe for a renovation project? +

Depending on scope, complete residential interior projects typically take between 45 to 90 days from design approval to final handover.
