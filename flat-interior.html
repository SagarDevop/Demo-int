FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Flat Renovation
Inquiries & Answers

Everything you need to know about our flat interior design, space optimization, DDA & society flat renovation, and timeline guarantees.

Have more questions? Call +91 98106 98082 →
What services does 4 Lotus Interior offer for flat interiors in Delhi-NCR? ▼

We specialize in turnkey flat interior design, full apartment renovation, space-saving modular furniture, parallel kitchens, bathroom overhauls, and lighting solutions across Dwarka, Noida, Gurgaon, and Delhi.

How do you optimize space in compact 2BHK & 3BHK flats? ▼

We utilize intelligent space planning — including hydraulic storage beds, sliding wardrobe doors, floor-to-ceiling cabinetry, slim false ceilings, and wall-hung furniture to make rooms feel expansive.

Can you renovate older DDA & society flats without structural risk? ▼

Yes! Our team conducts careful non-structural wall assessments, rewiring, plumbing overhauls, and ceiling updates while adhering to society maintenance rules and safety guidelines.

What is the typical timeline for a flat interior renovation project? ▼

A complete 2BHK or 3BHK flat interior design and turnkey fit-out takes between 35 to 60 days, backed by our strict on-time delivery guarantee.

How can I schedule a free site measurement & consultation for my flat? ▼

Call our direct studio hotline at +91 98106 98082 or submit an online request to arrange a site measurement visit by our senior interior architect.
