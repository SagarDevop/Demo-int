FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?
We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our commercial interior design, turnkey project execution, MEP engineering, timelines, and pricing transparency.

Have more questions? Call +91 98106 98082 →
What banquet hall interior design services do you offer in Delhi-NCR? ▼

We provide complete design and renovation for wedding banquet halls, convention centers, hotel ballrooms, and event venues — including false ceilings, acoustic wall paneling, bridal suites, buffet counters, and DMX lighting grids across Delhi, Gurgaon, and Noida.

How do you solve acoustic echo issues in large double-height banquet halls? ▼

We engineer custom micro-perforated acoustic wall paneling, fabric stretch ceilings, and sound-absorbing wall drapes with NRC ratings above 0.90 to eliminate echo during speeches and music.

Do you manufacture custom stage backdrops, buffet counters, and wall panels in-house? ▼

Yes! All CNC carved wooden wall panels, marble buffet service counters, stage steps, and bridal suite vanity furniture are manufactured at our Kirti Nagar workshop.

How do you handle heavy electrical loads for wedding lighting and sound systems? ▼

Our MEP team installs dedicated 3-phase high-amp electrical distribution panels, automatic DG generator sync switches, and commercial HVAC ducting.

What is the typical timeline for a banquet hall fit-out project in Delhi-NCR? ▼

A standard 5,000 to 15,000+ sq ft banquet hall fit-out takes 50 to 80 days from site handover to inauguration.
