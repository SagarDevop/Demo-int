FAQ

Modular Kitchen FAQs

Everything you need to know about materials, costs, installation timelines, and warranties in Delhi-NCR.

How long does a complete modular kitchen remodel take? +

Factory fabrication takes 12-18 days. On-site installation, stone fitting, and ducting are completed within 3 to 5 days.

What materials do you use for moisture & termite protection? +

We strictly use IS:710 Grade Boiling Waterproof Plywood (BWP) or High-Density Moisture-Resistant (HDMR) boards with hot-pressed acrylic edge banding.

What brands of German hardware do you install? +

We are certified partners for Blum and Hettich, featuring soft-close tandem boxes, org-line dividers, and hydraulic lift-up flap fittings.

Can you demolish existing civil platform slabs? +

Yes, we provide complete turnkey demolition, waste disposal, re-tiling, plumbing re-routing, and electrical conduit upgrades.

What is the warranty on 4 Lotus modular kitchens? +

We offer a 10-year structural warranty on all carcase boxes and a lifetime warranty on German hardware fittings.
